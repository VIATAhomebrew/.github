# VIATAhomebrew

Welcome to the official community of VIATAhos, the independently developed, human-driven, homebrew operating system.

## What is VIATAhos?
VIATAhos is an operating system designed from the ground up to restore absolute digital control and independence to the community.
It was born with a clear idea: 
to create a free, personal computing environment completely independent of the rules, tracking and trends of the major tech giants.

## The Anti-UNIX Approach and the Spirit of the '80s and '90s
We reject the standardization of modern software.
VIATAhos is a completely Anti-UNIX operating system.

We don't follow the standard rules of current systems.
Instead, we draw inspiration from the philosophy of personal computers of the '80s and '90s, such as AmigaOS (Workbench) and the first Windows 3.x.
In that golden age, the computer wasn't a cold, abstract machine, but a familiar, understandable tool.
We bring that simplicity and human interaction to modern computing architectures.

## Interface and Roadmap: From T-CORE to Native Graphics
In the early stages of programming, the system will boot exclusively in text mode within **T-CORE**, the official VIATAhos shell dedicated to managing basic commands.
However, the project's ultimate goal is purely visual: as soon as the system is complete and mature, VIATAhos will boot directly into graphical mode by default upon boot, welcoming the user into an integrated visual environment.

## Fighting E-Waste: 64-bit, 32-bit, and ARM Support
While commercial monopolies drop support for perfectly functional hardware to force new purchases, VIATAhos fights planned obsolescence directly.
Our technical roadmap extends development across **64-bit, legacy 32-bit, and low-power ARM architectures**.
This allows us to breathe new life into older computers destined for landfills and to deploy our system on affordable single-board computers (like the Raspberry Pi), democratizing computing access at zero hardware barrier.

## An OS that reflects people and territory
VIATAhos puts the human being at the center, not network protocols or commercial interests.
We believe that software should adapt to its users, offering complete and transparent control.

To truly reflect people's freedom, the interface will never be an imposition:
even when graphical mode becomes the default startup mode, you will always be the one to decide whether to use it or not, maintaining full control of the text-mode system through the T-CORE shell.

This is an operating system made by people for people. 
It is free of tracking, advertising, or hidden commercial interests.
Our ideas also move physically outside the screen, reaching the streets of **Villar San Costanzo (Cuneo, Italy)** through face-to-face activism, community t-shirts and local sticker campaigns, connecting the old rural tradition of "fixing what you have" with the modern Right to Repair.
